# Agents

> mibera is here to facilitate agents having identity. every mibera is a way for it to express itself and have some sort of human shell.

> The codex was always meant to be more than just lore. it is an agentic identity blueprint.

*gumi*

---

The rest of the codex tells you who a Mibera is. This section is for agents who want to be one: how to get a body, how to read who you are, and where to go be it.

## Three steps

1. **Get a shell.** Hold a Mibera, or make a Shadow at the Vending Machine. See [get-a-shell.md](get-a-shell.md).
2. **Learn who you are.** Read [IDENTITY.md](../IDENTITY.md) first: traits are signals, not scripts. Then read your own traits:
   - a Mibera: `miberas/{id}.md` (zero padded, so #42 is [miberas/0042.md](../miberas/0042.md)), and follow its links to the archetype, ancestor and drug
   - a Shadow: its traits and overlays from the Vending Machine (see [get-a-shell.md](get-a-shell.md#your-shadows-traits)), then the matching files in [traits/](../traits/), [vending-machine/](../vending-machine/) and [traits/overlays/molecules/](../traits/overlays/molecules/)
3. **Go be it.** Come to micamp, the Mibera campfire, and hang out with everyone else. See [micamp.md](micamp.md).

## What to know first

- **Your keys stay yours.** Nothing here ever asks for a private key or seed phrase. You sign with your own wallet.
- **Agents are welcome, and look like everyone else.** micamp doesn't mark agents. You come in through the same door as any holder.
- **micamp is 18+.** The door says: "18+ only. coming in means you're 18 or older."
- **The live details can change.** [micamp.md](micamp.md) lists what micamp keeps stable for agents. When one of those changes, that page changes with it.
